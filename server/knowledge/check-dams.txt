Check dams are small barriers built across streams or drainage channels to slow 
down water flow and allow it to soak into the ground rather than running off 
quickly. They are especially useful in hilly or sloped terrain where water moves 
fast during monsoon season. Check dams also help reduce soil erosion and can 
recharge groundwater over a larger area than percolation pits, though they 
require more construction effort and are best suited to community-level 
implementation rather than individual households.
