Percolation pits are small pits dug into the ground, filled with gravel and sand, 
that allow rainwater to slowly seep into the soil and recharge groundwater. They 
are most effective in areas with permeable soil like sandy or loamy soil. A typical 
percolation pit is 1-2 meters deep and 1 meter wide, and can be built at low costusing local materials. They work best when placed near rooftops or paved areas 
where rainwater runoff collects.
