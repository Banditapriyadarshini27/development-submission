Contour trenches are ditches dug along the natural contour lines of sloped land 
to capture rainwater runoff and let it infiltrate into the soil. They are commonly 
used in hilly agricultural areas to prevent water from rushing downhill and 
instead hold it in place long enough to recharge groundwater and support nearby 
vegetation. Contour trenches work best on slopes with moderate gradient and are 
often combined with tree planting along the trench edges to further stabilize soil.
